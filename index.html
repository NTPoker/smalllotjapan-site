How it works

  1. Choose a design, weights and colors.
  2. Send your logo and quantity.
  3. We quote in writing from Japanese factories, with our fee shown separately.
  4. You approve and pay up front. A first sample is made, then production.
  5. Shipped from Japan with inspection photos.

Please note

The images above are concept renderings, not photographs of finished products. We are a new service with no completed orders yet and hold no stock; everything is made to order, and the first sample is produced after you accept a quote and pay. Final shapes, weights and finishes are confirmed with the factory in each written quote. Mold fees are one-time and non-refundable. Factories publish small-lot terms on their own websites (for example, Sea Falcon lists OEM from 100 pieces; KATO SEIKO lists no minimum order and a one-time mold fee of roughly JPY 20,000–50,000). Lead-free options (tungsten, tin, steel) are available on request.
