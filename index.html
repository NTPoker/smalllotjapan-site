How it works

  1. Send your spec — product, weight, color, quantity, target price, destination.
  2. We get quotes from Japanese factories and send you the options in English, with lead times.
  3. You confirm and pay up front. Production starts only after payment is confirmed. Mold fees and custom items are non-refundable.
  4. Inspection photos from the factory before shipping.
  5. Direct shipment from Japan to you. Duties and import taxes are paid by the buyer.

Things to know

  • We are new and do not hold stock. Everything is made to order.
  • Lead-free options (tungsten, tin, steel, low-lead copper alloys) are available on request and priced separately. The EU is phasing in a ban on lead in fishing lures and sinkers; check your market's rules.
  • We do not sell, rank or promote brands for money. Quotes show the factory price and our fee separately.
